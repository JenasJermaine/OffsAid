Verifying the agent is not enough.

Kenyan footballers pursuing overseas trials face fraudulent offers because the evidence needed to check an opportunity is scattered: the agent, the letter, the messages, and what other players have already seen.

Who it is for

Kenyan footballers, 18 and over, pursuing opportunities abroad through informal channels.

Meet OffsAid

Three checks, one chat.

Run one check or all three, in any order. When you are done, OffsAid sends a summary of everything it found.

Agent verification

Is the agent on the licensed agents list? Send their name, licence number or phone number.

@agent-check

Document verification

Send the offer letter, trial invitation, contract or payment request. We check what it claims against public sources.

@document-check

Scam message flagging

Send a screenshot of your chat with the agent. We point out wording that often comes with scams, quoting the exact line.

What makes it different

Verification without leaving WhatsApp

Players already receive offers on WhatsApp. They can check them in the same place, in a few messages.

Pattern matching across cases

Each check makes the next one stronger: a number or letter reused under a new club's name is caught.

Flags risk, does not fake certainty

We show the evidence and quote the source. "Not confirmed" never means fake, and a licence never means an offer is real.
